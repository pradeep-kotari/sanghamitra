April 2009 · Volume 3, Issue 2 · by SuSri. From the Sanghamitra online magazine.

Welcoming Spring!

We have seen trees during winter - without leaves, shivering of cold. Now again they are getting tender leaves and the nature is turning colorful. The migrated birds coming back, people settled at home in warm room temperatures again coming out to experience the warm weather outside, the surroundings like a white carpet (during winter snow covered surroundings) turning green with fresh grass are specialities of this season. We can’t find a person who does not enjoy this situation. Let us all welcome spring with roses!

We celebrate Sri Rama Navami on the 9th day of the new year. In Bhadrachalam temple they celebrate Sitarama kalyanam every year on this day. Rama showed the mankind how to lead life. He himself practiced values and even in tough times he never gave up his values. Sita and Rama, best example of a married couple, showed mankind how to lead married life by sharing love, developing understanding, building trust and respecting each other. On wedding cards we see Rama & Sita to remind the married couple about their responsibilities, love, trust and respect.

In a family, all the members have good understanding of each other and help each other, then heaven is possible on earth itself. Let us all try to spend quality life and have good understanding of our family members. We can extend our family by including neighbours and then the members in that colony and so on. Then universal brotherhood is in our hands. Wishing you all a happy new year.

Let us meet again in July issue. Till then good bye! - SuSri.
