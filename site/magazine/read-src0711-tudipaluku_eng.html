Sanghamitra Concluding word July 2011 Edition

Concluding word

Dear readers,

Sanghamitra received lots of letters encouraging the team and sending wishes. Thank you for your wishes and we are getting motivation from your feedback time to time. We tried our best to bring the magazine with the articles you anticipate. We received some comments to improve the magazine. We are trying to incorporate those changes to make it interesting.

We received very few mails or letters answering the puzzles etc. It may be because of the one year gap. Please let your friends and neighbours know about Sanghamitra.

Send your answers before September 15th either by E-mail or Postal mail. Encourage your kids to do Kids’ Xword and other challenges. You may also send some quiz or crossword puzzles for the kids/adults. We will review it and post it in Sanghamitra.

As per our ancestors ‘Knowledge is like water in a well. It gets filled when you use it. You are not going to lose when you share the knowledge with others.’ We request you to send articles that you think may be useful for the readers. Send any articles or your feedback to ainapura@yahoo.com or susri@sanghamitra.org.

Note : Answers should be sent on or before September 15th.

Sanghamitra Team. Our Address: Editor, Sanghamitra, 1620 Strecker Ridge Ct., Wildwood, MO 63011.

Phone : (314) 395-9516

E-mail : susri@sanghamitra.org or ainapura@yahoo.com
