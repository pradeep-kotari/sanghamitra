How it works

  1. Save your details or a sitting — the live site updates immediately. Pradeep gets an email so he knows what changed.
  2. Upload poetry, a magazine issue, or a photo — it appears on the public page straight away. Pradeep gets an email.
  3. Messages from visitors — you and Pradeep both get email when someone fills a form. Reply here or on WhatsApp.
  4. Ask Pradeep to build something — for a new page or a bigger change. That does not go live until he builds it. He gets an email with your note. Or pick from What to build next below.
