The work, named plainly.

These are the doors people ask for first. If you want a private hour or a seat in a group session, say which one.

Harikatha

Harikatha is story, music, and teaching in one form. Sanghamitra hosts and shares these sessions so families can watch from home and scholars can be heard beyond a single hall.

Watch recordings Ask for a live session

Burrakatha

Burrakatha is the folk narrative Sanghamitra names among the arts it wants kept in circulation — not only archived, but offered to the community.

Ask when the next one is

Medhavadhanam

A live showcase of mental mathematics: no paper, pencil, or calculator. The first public Medhavadhanam was in 2007. Classes sit behind it — Vedic mathematics for students, and the Srinivasa Ramanujan Mathematricks Club.

Sundara Kanda

Readings and Harikatha from the Ramayana, offered as a regular practice. The next public sitting is 5 September: Sundara Kanda Harikatha by Srimati Jayanti Savitri, composed from the Kanda Ramayanam.
