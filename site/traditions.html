Ancient art forms

Encouraging the older forms.

Harikatha, Burrakatha, Sundara Kanda, dance. If you want a private hour or a seat in a group session, say which one.

Harikatha

Harikatha is story, music, and teaching in one form. Sanghamitra hosts and shares these sessions so families can watch from home and scholars can be heard beyond a single hall.

Burrakatha

Burrakatha is the folk narrative Sanghamitra names among the arts it wants kept in circulation — not only archived, but offered to the community.

Medhavadhanam

A live showcase of mental mathematics: no paper, pencil, or calculator. The first public Medhavadhanam was in 2007. Classes sit behind it — Vedic mathematics for students, and the Srinivasa Ramanujan Mathematricks Club. The latest public sitting was 22 August 2026 at the Hindu Temple of St. Louis.

The base of Sanghamitra is caring, sharing, and love.
— Sreenivasa, opening Medhavadhanam 2014
