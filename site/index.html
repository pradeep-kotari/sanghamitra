    What we do

    Enroll, give, join, or invite.

    One organization — eight areas of work. Pick the door that matches why you came.

    1. Sharing and caring

      Food, clothing, school fees and books, and support for elders.

    2. Ramanujan Mathematricks Club

      Vedic mathematics, school tutoring, SAT and ACT.

    3. Telugu

      Taught through SiliconAndhra Manabadi.

    4. Poetry

      One Satakam published; others in progress.

    5. Knowledge sharing

      Sittings on YouTube, Facebook and WhatsApp.

    6. Meditation, anger, stress

      Camps and community presentations.

    7. The older arts

      Harikatha, Burrakatha, Sundara Kanda, dance.

    8. The magazine

      An online quarterly, 2003 to 2011.
