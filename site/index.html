Sanghamitra సంఘమిత్ర

Sanghamitra is an organization founded in 2003 based on values, love, sharing and caring. Education for progress. It's a platform to learn, share and progress.

Sreenivasa Ainapurapu started it in St. Louis and runs all of it himself. Mathematics classes for children, talks and performances you can join from home, and Medhavadhanam, where his students do mathematics on stage without a calculator. Sanghamitra has never taken a donation.

Named Sanghamitra in 2003 · first public Medhavadhanam in 2007
