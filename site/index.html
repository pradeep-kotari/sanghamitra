    What we do

    Six kinds of work, one organization.

    Pick the door that matches why you came.

    1. Math Tutoring

      Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry and more.

    2. Knowledge Sharing

      Sittings on YouTube, Facebook and WhatsApp.

    3. Vedic Mathematics

      Summer program for ages 9 and above.

    4. Community Service

      Donations for food, books and clothing. Teaching Telugu. Presentations.

    5. Works of Literature

      Friend messages, poetry and articles.

    6. Workshops

      Meditation, anger and stress management.
