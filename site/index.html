The organization.

Sanghamitra does the work. Sreenivasa Ainapurapu founded it and still teaches, speaks, and hosts.

Parents come for Vedic mathematics, SAT-facing math, and Telugu. Others come to donate or volunteer, to invite a talk on meditation, anger, or stress, or because they read his poetry. Everyone can write him from this site.

What we do

Community work for more than 25 years.

Food and care for those who need it. Mathematics and Telugu for students. Poetry, meditation, the older arts, and sittings you can join from home.

  1. Sharing and caring

    Food donation, clothing, fee payment and books for unprivileged students, financial and moral support to elders.

  2. Srinivasa Ramanujan MathemaTRICKS Club

    Vedic mathematics summer program, middle- and high-school math tutoring, ACT and SAT coaching.

  3. Telugu

    Teaching through SiliconAndhra Manabadi.

  4. Writing poetry

    One Satakam published last year. Many others in progress.

  5. Knowledge sharing

    Sessions on Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, and this site.

  6. Meditation, anger, stress

    Meditation camps. Anger-management and stress-management presentations.

  7. Ancient art forms

    Encouraging the older forms — Harikatha, Burrakatha, Sundara Kanda, dance.

  8. Sanghamitra quarterly magazine

    Online, for knowledge, wisdom, and fun. 2003–2011.
