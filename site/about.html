The organization

A friend of society, not a personal homepage.

Sanghamitra (సంఘమిత్ర) has been a community organization since the early 2000s. The work is sharing: Vedic mathematics, Telugu, meditation, literature, and the older art forms, offered as one family — Vasudhaika Kutumbam.

Sreenivasa Ainapurapu founded it and still leads the sessions. The site is for people who want to know what is offered and take a next step: watch, sit in a group gathering, or book time.

The public trail is long: a quarterly magazine into 2011, Medhavadhanam showcases from 2007, temple and community programs, and a YouTube channel so you can watch from home.
