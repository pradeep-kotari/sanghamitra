The organization

A friend of society — from St. Louis or from your living room.

Sanghamitra (సంఘమిత్ర) was named in 2003. The first public Medhavadhanam was in 2007. Community work came long before the name. Sreenivasa Ainapurapu founded it and still leads the sittings.

The work is sharing: Vedic mathematics, Telugu, meditation, literature, and the older art forms, offered as one family — Vasudhaika Kutumbam.

The public trail is long: Medhavadhanam showcases from 2007, temple and community programs, Facebook, YouTube, and WhatsApp so you can watch and sit from home.

Friendship is a relation with no age bound. Even a two-year-old and someone of eighty can be friends. That is why the name Sanghamitra — friend of society.
— Sreenivasa, Medhavadhanam 2014, Part 1

What we do

The work under Sanghamitra.

Named in 2003 · first public Medhavadhanam in 2007.

  1. Sharing and caring

    Food donation, clothing, fee payment and books for students who cannot otherwise afford them, and financial and moral support to elders. Give toward this work, or volunteer.

  2. Srinivasa Ramanujan Mathematricks Club

    Vedic mathematics summer program, middle- and high-school math tutoring, ACT and SAT coaching. Enroll a student.

  3. Telugu

    Teaching through SiliconAndhra Manabadi. Ask for a seat.

  4. Writing poetry

    He writes. One Satakam was published last year; others are in progress. Readers, start here.

  5. Knowledge sharing

    Sessions on Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, and this site. Watch · Facebook · WhatsApp group

  6. Meditation, anger, stress

    Meditation camps, and presentations on anger management and stress management. Invite a sitting.

  7. Ancient art forms

    Encouraging the older forms: Harikatha, Burrakatha, Sundara Kanda, dance, and other traditional arts. Harikatha and the old arts.

  8. Sanghamitra quarterly magazine, 2003–2011

    The online quarterly for knowledge, wisdom, and fun, 2003–2011. Browse the archive.
