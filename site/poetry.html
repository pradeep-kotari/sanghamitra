Friend Messages

Every Friday, for two and a half years.

When he worked in a large company he sent a short message to his colleagues every Friday: an ethical thought, a small story from life, a lesson. It began as the Friday weekend message; take FRI from Friday and END from weekend and you have Friend. Senior people asked to be added to the list. He wrote them in Telugu and in English, and he still means to compile them into a booklet.

None are online yet. When Sreenivasa uploads them from his console they appear here, in the order he sent them.
