Give

He does not take money. Here is how to give anyway.

Sanghamitra has never accepted a donation. When an audience presses money on a Harikatha or Burrakatha artist after a sitting, every dollar goes to the artist. When a child in India needs school fees, the fee is paid to the school, never handed to a family. What Sreenivasa asks of people is older than any of this: give a tenth of what you earn back to society, and give it somewhere you are sure of.

Give where he is sure

If you want to give and do not know whom to trust, write to him. He points people only to organizations he has personally checked, in the United States and in India, where the money reaches the person it is meant for. Nothing passes through Sanghamitra.

Sponsor a child's school fees

Sanghamitra pays fees for children in India who cannot pay them. The money goes to the school, after he has spoken to the school, and people he trusts there check each case. If you want to carry one child's year, say so below.

Volunteer

The India Post piece thanks the volunteers who held the summer program and the Medhavadhanam. Hall setup, Zoom, Telugu class help, books for a library in India, hosting a talk in your city. Say what you can do.

What the work actually covers

Food and clothing drives. School fees and books, paid direct. Support for elders. Telugu classes. Books for libraries in Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam that people he knows keep running.
