Give

Money, or time. Both keep the sittings open.

Sanghamitra’s sharing and caring work includes food donation, clothing, school fees and books, and support for elders. This page does not take a card. Say you want to give; Sreenivasa will send how. If you already have a way, use it and leave your name below. If you need a receipt or a tax letter, ask when you write.

How to give

Pick a suggested amount to pre-fill your note — this is not a checkout. A Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal target appears here only after he adds it in admin. Until then, write or WhatsApp him.

What the gifts go toward

Food. Clothing. School fees and books. Elders. The summer programme and the hall, when that is what is needed.
