2004–2016 · సంఘమిత్ర

Sanghamitra’s online quarterly.

A quarterly online magazine meant for knowledge, entertainment and progress — that was the line on every masthead. It ran from October 2004 to April 2016, at first with Telugu and English side by side, then from October 2007 as separate Telugu and English editions, because a joke in Telugu does not survive translation.

25 issues, two of them known only by name. 479 pieces listed in his own tables of contents, of which 75 have been recovered from the Internet Archive and can be read here. The rest are named honestly and marked as not recovered.
