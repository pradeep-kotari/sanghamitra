## 10. Facts about the organization worth putting on the site

- Sanghamitra has **no other members** — he runs it alone. Harikatha and Burrakatha performers
  are collaborators, not staff. (15:05)
- He **takes no money for himself**. Audience gifts of $25–30 after a Harikatha go entirely to
  the artist. He has never accepted donations. (15:16)
- He publishes a performance to YouTube **only after asking the performer**, because reusing
  the material later matters to them. (16:31)
- The work predates the 2003 name — he paid schoolfees for students and tutored from high
  school onward, following his brother's example. (13:06, 14:14)
- His own framing of the values, worth quoting on the About page: love means a mother's love,
  given without expecting return, and the point is to pass the same to society. (10:20)
- Vedic culture's ten percent — he asks people to return a tenth of earnings to society, and
  will point them at organizations he has personally verified rather than take the money. (18:52, 19:11)
- For school fees he **pays the school directly** rather than handing money over, to prevent
  misuse. He maintains a network in India to check. (19:50)
